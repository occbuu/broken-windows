## Description

This software archive contains the reproducible code and generated results for a comparative analysis of municipal 311 service requests in New York City and Chicago.

The workflow covers raw-data validation and preparation, complaint-domain construction, non-negative matrix factorization of complaint vocabularies, tract and month fixed-effects models, municipal-responsiveness interactions, spatial diagnostics and spatial models, machine-learning prediction, SHAP explainability, heterogeneous-effect estimation, house-value cross-lags, crime cross-lags for New York City, and a Chicago robustness suite.

The archive includes Python scripts, cleaned Jupyter notebooks, machine-readable CSV and JSON result tables, figures, dependency specifications, derived-data schemas, and data-source instructions. It intentionally excludes the manuscript, reviewer documents, raw microdata, analysis-ready Parquet files, virtual environments, caches, and temporary logs. Raw inputs remain subject to the terms of their original public data providers.
